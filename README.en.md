## 📖 About

**Ostayabi** is a modern online platform designed to simplify the process of finding qualified construction professionals in Iran.

Whether you're planning a home renovation, building a new property, or looking for a specialist to complete a small repair, Ostayabi helps you discover experienced professionals in just a few clicks.

The platform connects customers with verified specialists from dozens of construction-related professions while helping contractors increase their online visibility and receive more project opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to modernize the construction services industry by creating a transparent, searchable, and reliable marketplace where skilled professionals and customers can connect safely and efficiently.
